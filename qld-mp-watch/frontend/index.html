How the numbers are built

evidence-first
Members come from Queensland Parliament. Sitting attendance comes from the official Record of Proceedings ATTENDANCE list. A recorded Aye/No vote can additionally confirm presence at that division, but a missed vote is never treated as proof of absence. Pairs are shown separately. Political gifts come from ECQ public disclosures and party-wide gifts are not allocated to individual MPs. Electorate polygons come from Queensland Government spatial data.
